## The lesson

Measuring a qubit always gives a plain 0 or 1. The probability of each is the square of its amplitude’s size, |α|² and |β|², which is called the Born rule. Measurement collapses the superposition: afterwards, the qubit is just the value you saw. A single outcome can’t be predicted, only the statistics over many identically prepared qubits.
