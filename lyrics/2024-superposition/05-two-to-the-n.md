## The lesson

Describing n qubits takes 2ⁿ amplitudes, so around fifty qubits already outgrow what ordinary computers can easily simulate. But a quantum computer doesn’t simply try every answer at once: measuring it gives you just one outcome. Quantum algorithms have to use interference to make the answer you want the likely one.
