## The lesson

Entangled qubits share one joint state that can’t be split into separate states for each qubit. A Hadamard gate followed by a CNOT creates the Bell state (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2: measure one qubit and you instantly know the other will match, however far apart they are. It still can’t be used to send a message faster than light. Einstein called it “spooky action at a distance.”
