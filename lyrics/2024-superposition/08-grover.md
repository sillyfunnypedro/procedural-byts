## The lesson

Grover’s algorithm searches an unsorted collection of N items in about √N steps instead of N. It starts with every item in an equal superposition, uses an “oracle” to flip the sign of the item you’re looking for, then reflects all the amplitudes around their average, which makes the marked one a little bigger. Repeating that about √N times makes it very likely to be measured. It’s a quadratic speedup, not an exponential one.
