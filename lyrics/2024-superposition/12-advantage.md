## The lesson

Quantum computers aren’t just faster general-purpose computers. They promise big speedups for particular problems: factoring, some kinds of search, and simulating molecules and materials. In 2019, Google reported a quantum processor beating classical supercomputers on one specially designed task, but today’s noisy, intermediate-scale machines haven’t yet delivered broad practical advantage. Expect hype, and ask for evidence.
