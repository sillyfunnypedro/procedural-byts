## The lesson

A classical bit is either 0 or 1. A qubit can be in a superposition of both, written α|0⟩ + β|1⟩, where α and β are amplitudes. It isn’t secretly one or the other, like a coin nobody has looked at yet; superposition is a genuinely different kind of state, and quantum algorithms are built to exploit it.
