## The lesson

Quantum gates are reversible operations on qubits, written as matrices (the same kind of matrix math as Matrix Transform). The Hadamard gate H turns |0⟩ into an equal superposition of 0 and 1, and applying it a second time brings back the original state, a small example of interference. X is the quantum NOT, and Z flips the phase. Unlike a classical AND gate, a quantum gate never throws information away.
