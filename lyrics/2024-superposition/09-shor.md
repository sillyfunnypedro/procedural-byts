## The lesson

Shor’s algorithm (1994) can factor huge numbers efficiently on a quantum computer, using the quantum Fourier transform to find the repeating period of a function. That would break RSA and similar public-key encryption. Quantum computers big and reliable enough don’t exist yet, but data stolen today could be decrypted later, so the world is moving to post-quantum cryptography: classical methods believed to resist quantum attacks. NIST published the first standards in 2024.
