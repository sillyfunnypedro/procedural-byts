## The lesson

Qubits are fragile. Any interaction with the environment, like heat, vibration or stray electromagnetic fields, effectively measures them and destroys superposition and entanglement. That’s decoherence. Quantum computers are cooled close to absolute zero and shielded, and quantum error correction spreads one reliable “logical” qubit across many physical qubits; useful fault-tolerant machines may need thousands of physical qubits for each logical one.
