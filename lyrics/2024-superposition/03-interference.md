## The lesson

Unlike probabilities, amplitudes can be negative (or even complex), so different paths through a computation can add up (constructive interference) or cancel out (destructive interference), the same effect that makes the stripes in the double-slit experiment. Quantum algorithms are designed so the paths leading to wrong answers cancel and the paths to the right answer reinforce, making the right answer likely when you measure.
