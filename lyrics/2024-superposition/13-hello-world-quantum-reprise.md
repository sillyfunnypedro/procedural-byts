## The lesson

The album comes back to the base case: every program starts with Hello, World, and every proof by induction needs one. A quantum state stays undecided until it’s measured, so the album ends unmeasured: whether Byt played the last note, and whether the duck quacked, stays in superposition. And the band’s names add up: a bit is 1 bit, a nybble 4, a byte 8, a word 32 and a quadword 64.
