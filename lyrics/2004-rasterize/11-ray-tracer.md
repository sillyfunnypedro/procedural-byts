## The lesson

Ray tracing draws a picture by shooting a ray from the eye through each pixel, finding the nearest surface it hits, and then shooting more rays: one toward each light to check for shadow, and reflected and refracted rays that bounce on recursively until a depth limit stops them, the base case. It produces accurate shadows, reflections and glass, but it’s expensive, so in 2004 real-time graphics used rasterization instead.
