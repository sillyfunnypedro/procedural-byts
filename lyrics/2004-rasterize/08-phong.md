## The lesson

Lighting models work out a surface’s color from the lights, the direction the surface faces (its normal) and where the viewer is. The Phong reflection model (1975) adds three parts: ambient (an even base glow), diffuse (brighter the more the surface faces the light, giving a matte look) and specular (a shiny highlight where light reflects toward the viewer). Flat shading uses one color per triangle, Gouraud shading blends corner colors, and Phong shading blends normals and lights every pixel.
