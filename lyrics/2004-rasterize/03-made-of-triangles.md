## The lesson

3D models are usually meshes of triangles, because three points always lie on one flat plane. Rasterization, also called scan conversion, fills each triangle by working out which pixels fall inside its three edges. Values stored at the corners, like color, depth and texture coordinates, are blended across the inside using barycentric coordinates. More triangles look smoother but take longer to draw.
