## The lesson

The graphics pipeline turns 3D geometry into pixels in stages: transform each vertex, assemble vertices into triangles, rasterize triangles into fragments (candidate pixels), shade and texture each fragment, then depth-test and blend the results into the framebuffer. GPUs run these stages massively in parallel, and by the early 2000s the vertex and fragment stages had become programmable through small programs called shaders.
