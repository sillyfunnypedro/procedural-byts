## The lesson

Aliasing happens when an image has more detail than the pixel grid can capture: jagged staircase edges (“jaggies”), edges that crawl when things move, strange moiré patterns, or wagon wheels that seem to spin backwards on film. Anti-aliasing takes several samples per pixel and averages them (supersampling), or filters before sampling, so edge pixels get in-between colors and look smooth.
