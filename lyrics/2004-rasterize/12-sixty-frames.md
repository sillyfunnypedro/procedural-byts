## The lesson

Animation is a sequence of still frames shown fast enough to look like motion, typically 24 to 60 or more per second; at 60 frames per second, each frame gets about 16.7 milliseconds. Double buffering draws the next frame into a hidden back buffer, then swaps it to the screen all at once to avoid flicker and tearing. Keyframe animation sets key poses and interpolates between them, and interactive programs run a loop: read input, update the world, render.
