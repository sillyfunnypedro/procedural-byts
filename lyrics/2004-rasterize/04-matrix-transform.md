## The lesson

Transformations move geometry around: translation moves it, rotation spins it and scaling resizes it. With homogeneous coordinates, where every point gets an extra fourth coordinate of 1, all three, including translation, become 4×4 matrix multiplications, so a whole chain of them can be combined into a single matrix. Matrix multiplication isn’t commutative, so the order of transformations matters.
