## The lesson

To draw a 3D scene you place a virtual camera, transform the world into the camera’s point of view, and project it onto a flat image. Perspective projection divides by depth, so distant things look smaller and parallel lines meet at a vanishing point; orthographic projection keeps sizes the same at every distance. The view frustum, cut off by a near and a far plane, is everything the camera can see.
