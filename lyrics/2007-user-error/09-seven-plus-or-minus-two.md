## The lesson

Short-term (working) memory is small. George Miller’s famous 1956 paper said about seven items, plus or minus two; later research suggests closer to four chunks. Chunking, grouping things together like the parts of a phone number, helps them fit. Good design keeps memory load low: let people recognize options instead of recalling them, as a menu does, and keep needed information on the screen.
