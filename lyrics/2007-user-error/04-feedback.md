## The lesson

Feedback tells people their action was received and what happened. Keeping the system’s status visible is the first of Jakob Nielsen’s usability heuristics. Rough guidelines: a tenth of a second feels instant, one second keeps someone’s train of thought, and ten seconds is the limit of attention, so anything longer needs a progress indicator. Without feedback, people repeat actions and make mistakes.
