## The lesson

Fitts’s law (1954) predicts that the time to point at a target grows with how far away it is and shrinks with how big it is. Important and frequent targets should be big and close. The edges and corners of a screen are especially easy to hit, because the pointer stops there, which makes them effectively infinitely large.
