## The lesson

In a think-aloud usability test, real users try realistic tasks while saying what they’re thinking. The observer doesn’t help, just notes where people hesitate, guess or get lost. Jakob Nielsen found that testing with about five users uncovers most of a design’s usability problems, so small, frequent tests work well.
