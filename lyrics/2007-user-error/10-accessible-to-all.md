## The lesson

Accessibility means people with disabilities can use a system. That includes text alternatives for images so screen readers can describe them, captions for audio, enough color contrast and never relying on color alone, everything usable from a keyboard, and clear structure. Guidelines like WCAG spell it out. Accessible design usually helps everyone, like curb cuts built for wheelchairs that help strollers and suitcases too.
