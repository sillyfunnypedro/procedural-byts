## The lesson

An affordance is what an object lets you do: a handle affords pulling, a flat plate affords pushing. A signifier is the visible cue that tells you so. Good design makes the right action obvious from the way things look. A door that needs a “push” sign because its handle says “pull” is called a Norman door, after Don Norman’s book The Design of Everyday Things.
