## The lesson

Prototyping tests a design idea before you build it. Low-fidelity prototypes, like paper sketches and index cards, are fast and cheap to change, and people give more honest feedback on something that looks unfinished. Higher fidelity comes later. Design is a cycle: design, prototype, test, learn, and go around again.
