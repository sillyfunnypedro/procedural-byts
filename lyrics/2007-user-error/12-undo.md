## The lesson

Good design prevents errors before they happen, with constraints and safe defaults, and asks for confirmation only before actions that can’t be reversed, because people stop reading “are you sure?” boxes that appear for everything. It also makes recovery easy: undo lets people explore without fear. Two of Nielsen’s heuristics are error prevention and user control and freedom.
