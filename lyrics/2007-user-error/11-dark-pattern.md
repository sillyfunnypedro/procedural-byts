## The lesson

Dark patterns are designs that trick people into things they didn’t mean to do: pre-checked boxes, cancellation hidden away, guilt-trip buttons like “No thanks, I like paying full price,” and fake countdown timers. They use what designers know about people against them. Ethical design serves the user’s real interests. The term was coined by Harry Brignull in 2010.
