## The lesson

People build a mental model of how a system works, and when it doesn’t match how the system really works, they make mistakes, like cranking a thermostat all the way up thinking the room will heat faster. Designers should make the system’s workings clear, stay consistent, and use natural mapping, where controls are laid out like the things they control, such as stove knobs arranged like the burners.
