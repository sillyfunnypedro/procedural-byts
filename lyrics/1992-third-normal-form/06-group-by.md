## The lesson

Aggregate functions like COUNT, SUM, AVG, MIN and MAX summarize many rows into one value. GROUP BY splits rows into groups that share a value and aggregates each group. WHERE filters rows before they’re grouped, and HAVING filters the groups afterwards.
