## The lesson

Storing the same fact in more than one place causes anomalies. Update anomaly: you change one copy and the others go stale. Insertion anomaly: you can’t record one fact without an unrelated one. Deletion anomaly: removing one fact accidentally removes another. Normalization removes the redundancy that causes all three.
