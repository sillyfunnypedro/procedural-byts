## The lesson

Entity-relationship modeling designs a database before you build it: entities (things, like bands, songs and shows) with attributes, and relationships between them with cardinalities: one-to-one, one-to-many or many-to-many. In the relational model, a table is a relation: a set of rows (tuples) with named columns, and the schema describes its shape.
