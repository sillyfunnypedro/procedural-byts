## The lesson

SQL is declarative: you say what you want, not how to get it. SELECT names the columns, FROM names the table, WHERE gives the condition rows must meet, ORDER BY sorts and DISTINCT removes duplicates. The database’s query optimizer works out the fastest way to get the answer.
