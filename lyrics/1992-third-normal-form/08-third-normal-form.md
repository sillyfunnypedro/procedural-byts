## The lesson

First normal form: every cell holds a single value, with no lists or repeating groups. Second normal form: every non-key column depends on the whole key, not just part of it. Third normal form: no non-key column depends on another non-key column. The usual summary is “the key, the whole key, and nothing but the key.” Split tables apart, link them by keys, and join them back together when you need to. Edgar Codd invented the relational model.
