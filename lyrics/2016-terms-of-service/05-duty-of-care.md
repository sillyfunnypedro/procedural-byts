## The lesson

Between 1985 and 1987, the Therac-25 radiation therapy machine gave massive overdoses to six patients, several of whom died. A software race condition, triggered when operators typed quickly, was one cause. The hardware safety interlocks of earlier models had been removed in favor of software checks, the error messages were cryptic, and early reports were dismissed. It’s the classic case for professional responsibility: safety-critical software needs layered safeguards, rigorous testing, clear error messages, and engineers who take every report seriously.
