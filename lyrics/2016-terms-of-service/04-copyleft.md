## The lesson

Open-source licenses use copyright to give people freedoms instead of taking them away. Permissive licenses like MIT, BSD and Apache let anyone reuse the code, even in closed products, as long as they give credit. Copyleft licenses like the GPL require that anyone who distributes a modified version shares it under the same license. “Free software” means free as in freedom, not price. Creative Commons licenses do the same for writing, music and images.
