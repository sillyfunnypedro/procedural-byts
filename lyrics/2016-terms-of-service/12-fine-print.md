## The lesson

Data protection laws give people rights over their personal data. The European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation, adopted in 2016 and enforced from 2018, includes the right to see your data, to correct it, to have it erased (the “right to be forgotten”) and to take it elsewhere, and it requires a lawful basis, like consent, for using it. Responsible professionals write clear terms, collect less, and respect these rights everywhere.
