## The lesson

Ethical frameworks give you different lenses for a hard decision. Consequentialism, including utilitarianism, judges an action by its outcomes. Deontology judges it by duties and rules, like never lying or treating people only as a means to an end. Virtue ethics asks what a person of good character would do. Careful ethical analysis looks through more than one lens, and the hardest cases are the ones where they disagree.
