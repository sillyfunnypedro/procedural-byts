## The lesson

Privacy matters because data points that seem harmless on their own, like location, contacts and browsing, can be combined to reveal intimate details of someone’s life. Good practice follows a few principles: collect only what you need (data minimization), use it only for the stated purpose, get informed consent, keep it only as long as necessary, and protect it. Surveillance, by companies or governments, can also discourage people from speaking freely.
