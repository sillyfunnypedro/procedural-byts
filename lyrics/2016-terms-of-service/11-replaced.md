## The lesson

Automation makes work more productive, but it can also displace workers, and the gains and costs are often shared unevenly. New kinds of jobs have historically appeared, but the transition hurts particular people and communities. Ethical computing asks who benefits, who pays, and what support people need, like retraining and safety nets.
