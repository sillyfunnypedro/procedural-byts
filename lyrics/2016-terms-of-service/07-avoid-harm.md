## The lesson

Professional codes of ethics, like the ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct (first adopted in 1992, revised in 2018) and the IEEE Code of Ethics, set out what computing professionals owe the public: contribute to society and human well-being, avoid harm, be honest and trustworthy, be fair, respect privacy and other people’s work, take on only work you’re competent to do, accept review, and put the public good first.
