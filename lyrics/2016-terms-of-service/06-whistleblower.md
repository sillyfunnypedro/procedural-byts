## The lesson

Whistleblowing means reporting wrongdoing or danger, inside an organization or outside it, when the normal channels fail. Professional codes put public safety first. Doing it responsibly means documenting the evidence, using internal channels first where possible, understanding the legal protections that exist, and weighing the personal cost honestly.
