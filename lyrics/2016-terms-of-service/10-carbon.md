## The lesson

Computing has environmental costs. Data centers use large amounts of electricity and water, making devices takes mined materials and energy, and electronic waste is a growing problem. Sustainable practice means writing efficient software, keeping hardware running longer and making it repairable, recycling responsibly, and running workloads where the electricity is cleaner.
