## The lesson

Technology reflects the people who build and test it. Teams made up of similar people can miss failures that affect everyone else, like automatic soap dispensers and image systems that work poorly on darker skin. Diverse, inclusive teams, testing with a wide range of people, accessible design, and involving the communities affected all lead to fairer, better products. Equity means giving people what they actually need, not just identical treatment.
