## The lesson

Terms of service and privacy policies are legal agreements that almost nobody reads; researchers have estimated it would take weeks of every year to read all the ones a typical person accepts. Meaningful, informed consent means people can actually understand what they’re agreeing to, so professionals should write terms clearly and never bury important uses of people’s data in the fine print.
