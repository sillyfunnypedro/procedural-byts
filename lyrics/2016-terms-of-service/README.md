# Terms of Service (2016)

*2016 post-metal. Society, ethics, and professionalism.*

Terms of Service is the 2016 album from Procedural Byts: twelve tracks of post-metal about what computing does to people, and what the people who build it owe everyone else. Nyble sings lead and plays bass, Qword plays layered, delay-drenched guitar, Wyrd builds atmospheric drones, and Bytes plays slow, crushing drums and shouts the backing vocals from a distance. Terms of service, privacy, copyright and open source, safety-critical failure, whistleblowing, codes of ethics, ethical frameworks, equity, the environmental cost of computing, automation, and data protection law. Written after the band read its 1970 record contract for the first time and found it had signed away the rights to the duck. Play loud. Read the fine print.
