## The lesson

Abstraction layers hide complexity behind simple interfaces, like files, sockets, virtual memory and SQL, and that’s how anyone builds large systems at all. But Joel Spolsky’s law of leaky abstractions (2002) says every non-trivial abstraction leaks: the hidden details, like latency, failures and performance, eventually show through. That’s why engineers need to understand at least a little of the layer underneath.
