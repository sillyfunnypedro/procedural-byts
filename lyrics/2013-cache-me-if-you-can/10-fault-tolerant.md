## The lesson

A fault-tolerant system keeps working when parts of it fail. The key is avoiding any single point of failure through redundancy: spare servers, disks, power supplies and network paths. Failures are detected and the system fails over to a backup automatically. The working assumption is that every component will eventually fail.
