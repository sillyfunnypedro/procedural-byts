## The lesson

Caching keeps a copy of data close to where it’s used, so you don’t have to make the slow trip every time. It shows up at every layer of a system: CPU caches, the operating system’s page cache, browser caches, DNS caches and content delivery networks. How well it works depends on the hit rate, and when the cache is full, a policy like least recently used decides what to throw out.
