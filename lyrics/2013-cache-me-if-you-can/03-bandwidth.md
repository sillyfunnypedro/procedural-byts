## The lesson

Bandwidth, or throughput, is how much data moves per second, and it’s different from latency. A truck full of hard drives has enormous bandwidth but a very long latency; Andrew Tanenbaum made the joke about a station wagon full of tapes. You raise throughput by keeping many operations in flight at once, through pipelining or parallel requests, even though each one still takes just as long.
