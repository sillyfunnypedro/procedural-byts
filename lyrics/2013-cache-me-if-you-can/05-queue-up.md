## The lesson

Queueing theory explains why busy systems get slow so suddenly: as a server’s utilization approaches 100%, the waiting time doesn’t rise steadily, it shoots up. Systems need slack. Little’s law ties it together: the average number of things in a system equals the arrival rate times the average time each one spends there. It applies to CPUs, disks, networks and checkout lines alike.
