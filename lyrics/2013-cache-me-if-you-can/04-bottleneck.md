## The lesson

A system is only as fast as its bottleneck, the most constrained resource, whether that’s the CPU, memory, disk, network or a single lock. Speeding up anything else barely helps. Measure with profilers and benchmarks instead of guessing, and expect a new bottleneck to appear as soon as you fix the old one.
