## The lesson

Availability is the fraction of time a system is working, usually counted in nines: 99% means about 3.65 days down per year, 99.9% about 8.8 hours, 99.99% about 53 minutes and 99.999% about 5 minutes. You improve it by making failures rarer (longer mean time between failures) and fixing them faster (shorter mean time to repair). Every extra nine costs far more than the last, so pick the level the service really needs.
