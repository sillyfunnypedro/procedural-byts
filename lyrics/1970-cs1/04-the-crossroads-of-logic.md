# The Crossroads of Logic

**CS1** (1970), track 4

[▶ Listen on Suno](https://suno.com/song/b244c0a5-2cb6-4ba5-bdf2-ace095f4c7f6)

## Lyrics

**[Intro (spoken)]**  
Every program reaches the crossroads… and it must choose its path.

**[Verse 1]**  
I stand at the fork where the dark roads divide,  
if the condition is true, I go to one side.  
else I take the other, there’s no in-between,  
True or false, the sharpest blade you’ve seen!  
else if, else if, I can chain them in a row,  
Checking every gate till I know where to go!

**[Chorus]**  
Decide! Decide! The branches are calling!  
One path is rising, the other is falling!  
Test it with if or switch on the sign,  
The program obeys, the choice is divine!

**[Verse 2]**  
When the question is a range, like greater than ten,  
When AND meets OR in the battle again,  
When the truths are tangled and they can’t be named,  
if is the hammer that won’t be tamed!

**[Verse 3]**  
Now the switch is a judge with a single decree,  
He takes one value and he sets it free.  
Case one, case two, case three in a row,  
He jumps to the match and he lets it go!  
And the default waits when no case is true,  
The last line of defense standing guard over you!

**[Guitar solo: Byt]**  
*Byt’s solo reaches a fork every four bars. Bytes calls the condition with a snare hit or a kick, and Byt takes the high road or the low road, never both: if/else. Then the switch section: Bytes shouts a number, Byt jumps straight to the matching lick out of four, and when the number matches none of them he lands on a held power chord: the default.*

**[Bridge]**  
Fall through! (Fall through!) Forgot to break the line!  
Fall through! (Fall through!) Now you’re running every case in time!  
Put your break at the end of the deed,  
Or the cases will bleed, the cases will bleed!

**[Final chorus]**  
if for the ranges! if for the twisted and the true!  
Switch for one value with exact cases to see you through!  
Decide! Decide! The branches are calling!  
The program obeys, or the program is falling!

## The lesson

Use if/else if/else for ranges and compound conditions; use switch when one value is compared against many exact options. In classic switch syntax, a missing break falls through to the next case.
