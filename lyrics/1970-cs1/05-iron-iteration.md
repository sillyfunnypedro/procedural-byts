## Lyrics

**[Intro over feedback (spoken)]**  
In the depths of the machine, where the instructions never sleep… there are three ways to repeat.

**[Verse 1]**  
I know where I’m starting, I know where I’ll end,  
A counter in my fist from one up to ten.  
for i = 1, and I march down the line,  
Each step is numbered, each step is mine!  
When the count runs out, I break from the chain,  
I know how many times I’ll ride through the pain!

**[Chorus]**  
Loop! Loop! Around and around!  
The code keeps on turning, it’s iron-bound!  
Count it, or check it, before or behind,  
Iteration is burning a hole in my mind!

**[Verse 2]**  
Now the while is a watchman who stands at the gate,  
He checks the condition before you can wait.  
While the fire is burning, he lets you inside,  
But if it’s already false, you’ll never ride!  
Zero times or forever, he won’t say which,  
Forget to change the test and you’re stuck in the ditch!

*[Chorus]*

**[Bridge]**  
Infinite loop! (Infinite loop!) No way out of the hall!  
Infinite loop! (Infinite loop!) Forgot to update it all!  
Change the variable, break the spell,  
Or spin forever in silicon hell!

**[Verse 3]**  
Repeat until: the reckless one,  
He charges through the door before the test is done.  
He does the deed once, then he asks the question,  
Until the dragon’s dead, no hesitation!  
At least one time he’ll swing the blade,  
The check comes after the move is made!

**[Drum solo: Bytes]**  
*Three movements. First, exactly ten bars of the same fill, counted out loud by the crowd: the for loop. Next he plays only while Nyble holds a note and stops the instant she lets go; some nights she never starts and he never plays a beat: the while loop. Last, he crashes in before anyone signals, plays one full fill, and only then looks to Byt to see whether to go again: repeat-until.*

**[Final chorus]**  
For when you know the number!  
While when you check before!  
Repeat until you’ve done it once,  
Then test it at the door!

## The lesson

for when the number of repetitions is known; while tests first and may run zero times; repeat-until (do-while) tests last and always runs at least once. Something inside the loop must move it toward stopping.
