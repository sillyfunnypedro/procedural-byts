## The lesson

Software runs on many platforms, like the web, phones, tablets, desktops, game consoles and tiny embedded devices, each with its own languages, rules and app stores. Native apps are fast and feel right on their platform but have to be written separately for each one. Web apps run anywhere there’s a browser. Cross-platform frameworks share most of the code, at some cost in speed or polish.
