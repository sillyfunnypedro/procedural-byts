## The lesson

Touch is not a mouse. Fingers are imprecise and cover up whatever they touch, so touch targets need to be big (platform guidelines suggest roughly 44 to 48 points) and spaced apart, with feedback shown around the finger. There’s no hover on a touchscreen. Gestures like swipe, pinch and long press are invisible, so they need cues before people will discover them.
