## The lesson

Embedded systems are computers built into other things, like appliances, cars and sensors, often small microcontrollers with very little memory, low power and no full operating system. They read sensors, drive actuators like motors, and often respond to events with interrupts. Real-time systems must meet deadlines, and in hard real-time systems, like car brakes, a late answer is a wrong answer.
