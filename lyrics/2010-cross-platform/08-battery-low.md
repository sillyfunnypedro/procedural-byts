## The lesson

Phones run on batteries, so energy is a real limit. The screen, the cellular and Wi-Fi radios and the GPS use the most power, and waking the phone up constantly stops it from ever sleeping deeply. Good apps batch their network requests, ask for location sparingly, work offline and sync later, and let the phone sleep.
