## The lesson

On a phone, the operating system controls an app’s life. Apps get created, started, resumed, paused when they go into the background, stopped, and may be killed without warning when the phone needs memory. An app has to save its state when it’s paused and restore it when the user comes back, so nobody ever loses their place.
