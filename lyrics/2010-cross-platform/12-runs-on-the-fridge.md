## The lesson

The Internet of Things connects everyday objects, like thermostats, lights, doorbells and appliances, to the network, each one a small embedded computer sensing and reporting. Every connected device is also a way in for attackers, so these devices need secure defaults, a unique password each, and firmware that can be updated.
