## The lesson

Embarrassingly parallel problems split into independent tasks that need little or no communication, like resizing thousands of photos or rendering separate frames of a film, so they speed up almost perfectly as you add processors. Data parallelism applies the same operation to many pieces of data at once; task parallelism runs different tasks at the same time.
