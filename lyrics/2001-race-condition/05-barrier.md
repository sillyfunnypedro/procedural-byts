## The lesson

A barrier is a synchronization point: every thread stops there and waits until all of them have arrived, then they all continue. It’s common in algorithms that work in phases. Because each phase lasts as long as the slowest thread, load balancing, giving every thread a similar amount of work, matters a lot.
