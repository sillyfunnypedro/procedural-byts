## The lesson

Consensus algorithms, like Paxos (1998), let a group of machines agree on a value even when some fail or messages get lost, usually by requiring a majority, called a quorum. With 2f + 1 machines, the group survives f failures. Replication keeps copies of data on several machines so one failure loses nothing. If a network split lets two groups each decide on their own, you get split brain.
