> After the night in the story, backstage passes needed two factors: the pass, and the answer to a question. The question is “what does the duck say?”

## The lesson

Multi-factor authentication asks for proof from two different categories: something you know (a password), something you have (a card, a token, a phone) and something you are (a fingerprint or face). Stealing one factor is no longer enough, which stops most password theft.
