## The lesson

Authentication establishes who you are; authorization decides what you’re allowed to do, often with access control lists or roles. The principle of least privilege gives every user and program only the permissions it actually needs, so if one is compromised the damage stays small. Don’t run things as an administrator just because it’s easier.
