## The lesson

A cryptographic hash function turns any data into a short, fixed-size fingerprint. It only works one way, and changing even one bit gives a completely different hash. A digital signature is made with the signer’s private key; anyone with the public key can verify who signed the message and that it hasn’t been changed since.
