## The lesson

Public-key (asymmetric) cryptography uses a pair of keys: anything locked with the public key can only be unlocked with the matching private key. Anyone can send you a secret without sharing one first. RSA’s security rests on how hard it is to factor the product of two huge primes. In practice, public keys are used to agree on a fast symmetric key for the rest of the conversation.
