## The lesson

Social engineering attacks people instead of technology: phishing emails, pretexting phone calls, following someone through a locked door, all using urgency and authority to get around good judgment. Defenses include training, verification steps (call back on a number you already know), and systems designed so one person’s mistake can’t compromise everything. People are part of the security system.
