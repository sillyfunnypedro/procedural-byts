## The lesson

Deadlock happens when processes each hold a resource while waiting for one another in a cycle. It needs four conditions at once: mutual exclusion, hold and wait, no preemption, and circular wait. Break any one, for example by always taking resources in the same order, and deadlock can’t happen. The dining philosophers are the classic example.
