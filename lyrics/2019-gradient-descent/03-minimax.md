## The lesson

For two-player games, minimax search assumes the opponent always plays their best: you choose the move whose worst possible outcome is the best. Game trees grow explosively, so alpha-beta pruning skips branches that can’t change the result, and searches stop at a set depth and score positions with an evaluation function. IBM’s Deep Blue beat world champion Garry Kasparov in 1997.
