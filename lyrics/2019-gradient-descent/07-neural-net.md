## The lesson

An artificial neural network is built from simple units, “neurons,” that each take a weighted sum of their inputs and pass it through a nonlinear activation function. They’re arranged in layers: input, hidden and output. A single layer can only separate data with a straight line, so it can’t even learn XOR, but hidden layers make networks far more powerful. Deep learning stacks many layers and trains them on huge datasets using GPUs.
