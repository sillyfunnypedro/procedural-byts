## The lesson

AI often has to reason under uncertainty. Bayes’ theorem updates a prior belief with new evidence to get a posterior belief: P(H | E) = P(E | H) × P(H) / P(E). Naive Bayes classifiers, like early spam filters, combine the evidence from many words, assuming each one is independent. Probabilistic reasoning keeps degrees of belief instead of pretending to be certain.
