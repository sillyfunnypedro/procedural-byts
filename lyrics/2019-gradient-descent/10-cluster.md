## The lesson

Unsupervised learning finds structure in data that has no labels. Clustering groups similar items together. The k-means algorithm picks k starting centers, assigns every point to its nearest center, moves each center to the middle of its group, and repeats until nothing changes. You have to choose k yourself, and it’s up to you to work out what the clusters mean.
