## The lesson

In 1950, Alan Turing proposed the imitation game: a judge has a text conversation with a person and a machine, and if the judge can’t reliably tell which is which, the machine passes. Critics point out that passing, sometimes with tricks like a 2014 chatbot pretending to be a teenager, doesn’t prove understanding; John Searle’s Chinese Room argument makes the same point. The test is still a useful way into the question of what “intelligence” means.
