## The lesson

In reinforcement learning, an agent learns by acting in an environment and getting rewards or penalties, with the goal of learning a policy that earns the most reward over time. It has to balance exploration, trying something new, against exploitation, doing what already works. DeepMind’s AlphaGo beat top Go player Lee Sedol in 2016 by combining reinforcement learning, neural networks and search.
