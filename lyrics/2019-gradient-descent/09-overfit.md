## The lesson

A model overfits when it learns its training data too closely, noise and all: it scores perfectly on the training data and badly on anything new. A model that’s too simple underfits. To check how well a model generalizes, hold back test data it never sees during training, plus validation data for tuning. Remedies include more data, simpler models, regularization and stopping training early.
