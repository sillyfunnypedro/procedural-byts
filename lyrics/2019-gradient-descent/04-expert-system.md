## The lesson

Symbolic AI represents knowledge explicitly, as logic and rules. Expert systems, popular in the 1970s and 1980s (MYCIN, for diagnosing infections, is a famous one), capture an expert’s if-then rules and use an inference engine to chain them to a conclusion, and they can explain exactly which rules they used. Their weakness is brittleness: they fail on anything outside their rules, and writing all those rules takes enormous effort.
