## The lesson

Generative models, like the language models that appeared around 2019 (GPT-2, for example), create text, images or music by predicting what’s likely to come next based on their training data. The output can be fluent and still wrong, which people call hallucination. These models absorb the biases and blind spots of their data, often can’t explain their answers, and raise real questions about consent and credit for the data they were trained on. People still have to check the work and take responsibility for it.
