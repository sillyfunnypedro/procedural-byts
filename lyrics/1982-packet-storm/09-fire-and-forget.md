## The lesson

UDP sends datagrams with no connection setup, no acknowledgments, no ordering and no retransmission. That means very little overhead and low delay, but no guarantee of delivery. It suits real-time voice, video and games, where late data is useless, and quick lookups. TCP trades speed for reliability and order.
