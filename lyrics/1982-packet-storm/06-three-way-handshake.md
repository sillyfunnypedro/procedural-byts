## The lesson

TCP sets up a connection with a three-way handshake: the client sends SYN with its starting sequence number, the server replies SYN-ACK with its own, and the client confirms with ACK. Both sides agree on numbering before any data flows. When they’re done, each side sends FIN to close the connection.
