## The lesson

TCP numbers every byte it sends. The receiver uses these sequence numbers to put data back in order and throw away duplicates, and sends acknowledgments (ACKs) saying what has arrived. Together they turn a network that can scramble and duplicate packets into an ordered, reliable stream.
