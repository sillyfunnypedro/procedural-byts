## The lesson

Networks are built in layers: the OSI model has seven (physical, data link, network, transport, session, presentation, application), and the internet uses the simpler TCP/IP stack. Each layer serves the one above it and uses the one below. Sending wraps the data in each layer’s header on the way down (encapsulation), and receiving unwraps them in reverse.
