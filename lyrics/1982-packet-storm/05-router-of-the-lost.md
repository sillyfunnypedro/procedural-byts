## The lesson

Routers forward packets hop by hop, using routing tables that map destinations to the next hop. Routing protocols let routers share what they know about links so they can find good (often shortest) paths and route around failures. Each packet carries a time-to-live counter that drops at every hop, so a lost packet can’t circle forever.
