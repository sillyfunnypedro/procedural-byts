## The lesson

The internet is a network of networks joined by shared protocols. On January 1, 1983, the ARPANET switched to TCP/IP all at once, a cutover known as flag day. Protocols are agreements about message formats and behavior, and client-server is the most common pattern: clients send requests and servers respond.
