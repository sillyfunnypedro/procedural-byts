## The lesson

Packet switching splits data into packets, each with a header saying where it came from, where it’s going and where it fits. Packets travel independently, possibly by different routes, and are reassembled at the far end. Unlike circuit switching, which reserves a whole line for one conversation like an old telephone call, packets share the links, which makes networks efficient and hard to knock out.
