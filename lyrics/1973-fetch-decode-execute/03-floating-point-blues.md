## The lesson

Real numbers are stored as floating point: a sign, an exponent and a mantissa. Many decimals, like 0.1, can’t be stored exactly in binary, so 0.1 + 0.2 isn’t exactly 0.3. Compare floats with a tolerance instead of ==. Precision is relative: the gaps between representable numbers grow as numbers get larger.
