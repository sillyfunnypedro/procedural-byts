## The lesson

Design patterns are named, reusable solutions to problems that come up again and again, popularized by the 1994 book by Gamma, Helm, Johnson and Vlissides, the “Gang of Four.” Observer notifies dependents when something changes, decorator wraps an object to add behavior, factory hides how objects are created, and strategy swaps one algorithm for another. Their names give a team a shared vocabulary.
