## The lesson

The waterfall model runs requirements, design, implementation, testing and deployment strictly in order. It’s easy to plan, but it finds problems late, when they’re most expensive to fix. Iterative and incremental models, like the spiral model, build the software in small cycles and get feedback early and often.
