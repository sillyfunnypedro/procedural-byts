## The lesson

Technical debt, a term from Ward Cunningham in 1992, is the future cost of shortcuts like quick fixes, skipped tests and messy structure. Like money, it can be worth borrowing on purpose to ship on time, but it charges interest: every later change gets slower until you pay it back by refactoring.
