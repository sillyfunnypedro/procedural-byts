## The lesson

Software estimates are famously optimistic. Breaking work into small, well-understood tasks and using data from past projects helps. The ninety-ninety rule jokes that the last 10% of the work takes as long as the first 90%. Brooks’s law, from The Mythical Man-Month (1975): adding people to a late project makes it later, because new people need time to learn and everyone spends more time communicating.
