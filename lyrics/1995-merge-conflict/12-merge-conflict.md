## The lesson

Merging combines changes from different branches. When two branches change the same lines, the tool reports a merge conflict, and a person has to resolve it by choosing one version or combining both. Merging often keeps branches small and conflicts rare. Most of software engineering is teamwork, and most conflicts are resolved by people talking to each other.
