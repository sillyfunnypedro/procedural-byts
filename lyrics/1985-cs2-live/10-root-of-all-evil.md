## The lesson

In a binary search tree, everything in the left subtree is smaller and everything in the right is larger. Search and insert take O(log n) when the tree is balanced but O(n) when it degenerates into a line (for example, inserting sorted data). Balanced trees use rotations, and an in-order traversal visits values in sorted order.
