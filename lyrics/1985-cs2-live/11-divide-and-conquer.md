## The lesson

Merge sort splits the list in half until each piece has one item, then merges the sorted halves: always O(n log n), with extra space. Quicksort partitions around a pivot and sorts each side: O(n log n) on average, O(n²) with bad pivots. Both are divide-and-conquer recursion.
