## The lesson

When outcomes are equally likely, probability is favorable outcomes over possible outcomes. Add probabilities for events that can’t both happen; multiply for independent events. Conditional probability updates on what you know. Dice have no memory, so “I’m due” is a fallacy. Expected value is the probability-weighted average, and variance measures how spread out the results are.
