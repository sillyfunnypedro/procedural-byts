## The lesson

a mod n is the remainder after dividing a by n, and two numbers are congruent mod n when they leave the same remainder, like hours on a clock. A prime has no divisors except 1 and itself. Euclid’s algorithm finds the greatest common divisor, and the least common multiple is when two cycles line up again. Modular arithmetic is the math behind hashing and cryptography.
