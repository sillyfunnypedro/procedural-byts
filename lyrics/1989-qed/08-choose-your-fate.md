## The lesson

Counting rules: for “this or that,” add (the rule of sum); for “this and then that,” multiply (the rule of product). There are n! orderings (permutations) of n items. Choosing k items when order doesn’t matter gives n choose k = n! / (k! (n − k)!) combinations, the numbers in Pascal’s triangle.
