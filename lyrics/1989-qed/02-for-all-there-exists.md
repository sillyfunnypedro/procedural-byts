## The lesson

“For all x, P(x)” has to hold for every x, and a single counterexample disproves it. “There exists an x with P(x)” needs just one example, called a witness. Negation swaps them: “not for all” means “there exists one that isn’t.” The order of mixed quantifiers matters: “every lock has a key” is much weaker than “one key opens every lock.”
