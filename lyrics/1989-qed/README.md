# Q.E.D. (1989)

*1989 progressive metal concept album. Mathematical and statistical foundations.*

Q.E.D. is the 1989 progressive metal concept album from Procedural Byts, and it is one long proof. In 1988 Byt flipped mid-solo and left the band, leaving a single line written on the bottom of the duck: if n, then n plus one. No base case. Nyble sings lead and plays bass, new member Qword plays shred lead guitar, Wyrd plays keyboards and orchestral synths, and Bytes plays drums and sings backup. Twelve songs cover the math of computing: logic, quantifiers, proof by contradiction, induction, sets, functions, the pigeonhole principle, counting, probability, modular arithmetic and graphs, until the band finds the base case in the first song they ever played. Then the duck quacks. Play loud. Show your work.
