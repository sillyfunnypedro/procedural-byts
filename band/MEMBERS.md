# The band

## Nyble

**Lead vocals, bass guitar**, 1970 to now

Four bits. Half a byte, one hex digit, and the voice of the band on every album.

## Byt

**Lead guitar**, 1970 to 1988, and 2024 in superposition

One bit. Flipped mid-solo in 1988; the band blames a cosmic ray. The band is named after him.

## Bytes

**Drums, backing vocals**, 1970 to now

Eight bits and eight flip-flops. Counts every loop out loud, raps, screams, growls, and was once told he could not be scaled up.

## Wyrd

**Synthesizers, keytar, organ, turntables, piano**, 1985 to now

Thirty-two bits. Answered a magazine ad that said “must align on word boundaries.” Remembers the exact bar Byt stopped on.

## Qword

**Lead guitar**, 1988 to now

Sixty-four bits. The sixty-fourth guitarist to audition. Went without guitar solos from 1992 to 2003 and has been making up for it since. Shares lead guitar with Byt on the 2024 reunion.

## Null

**Rubber duck, technical advisor**, Was there first

Never returns anything. Returned exactly one value, ever, at the end of Q.E.D. in 1989.
